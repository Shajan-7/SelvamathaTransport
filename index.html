Since 1998

Built on Faith, Family, & Unyielding Work Ethic

Selvamatha Transport was established in 1998 by S. Xavier. From a humble beginning overcoming severe logistics challenges in southern Tamil Nadu, he pioneered a service founded on a single non-negotiable motto: deliver every consignment safely, securely, and at the promised time.

"Send anything, anywhere, anytime — at the correct time."

Today, that proud legacy is sustained and driven forward by his brothers, S. Innasimuthu & S. Micheal — revered as the Two Pillars of Selvamatha Transport. Through decades of expanding road networks, our wheels have kept running relentlessly across Tamil Nadu and Kerala.
